Your crew

P
The Planner · the main agent — reads, judges, dispatches
Every request goes through the Planner first. It reads your memory when the question is about your life, judges the task's difficulty from rules you can read on the answer, and picks the cheapest model on Vertex AI that fits — none for a question your memory answers by itself, the smallest for a rewrite or a short answer, the standard for most, the deep one for planning and analysis. It spends no tokens deciding.
A
The Assistant · answers what you ask, on the fly
The member for unplanned tasks. Gets your request and, as data, the memories the Planner read; never invents a fact about your life; says plainly when your memory holds nothing relevant.
B
The Briefer · reads your memory, writes your brief
Reads people and meetings from the memory poppy you installed, through AgentsPoppy — never writes, never sends. Writes your brief with Gemini on Vertex AI inside this project, under your card and CrewPoppy's caps — or in its own words when the model is off, capped or away, and says which.

Tools, schedules and runs that outlive the app — as on AWS — arrive with the Google port, one release at a time.
